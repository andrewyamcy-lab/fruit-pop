How to play 🍒

  1. Drag anywhere inside the game box to aim.
  2. Release your finger to drop a fruit.
  3. When two matching balls touch, they become the next bigger face ball.
  4. Keep balls below the dashed line. Merge all 7 face balls for a huge score!
